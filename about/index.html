About the farm

A mushroom farm that does things properly

हमारी कहानी

Maba Agro is a Noida mushroom farm supplying fresh, organically grown mushrooms to wholesale markets, hotel kitchens and homes across North India.

Noida, Uttar Pradesh

Our story

Built around not cutting corners

Mushrooms are honest. Rush the compost, skip a step on hygiene, or leave a crate in the sun for an hour, and the mushroom shows it: brown caps, open gills, a slimy shelf.

We grow button, oyster, shiitake and portobello mushrooms in climate-controlled rooms, on natural substrates that we pasteurise with heat. We pick by hand at first light, grade carefully, and keep every crate cold until it’s in your hands.

Our mushrooms go to sabzi mandi wholesalers, to hotel kitchens including Lemon Tree, Radisson, Ginger, Sarovar, ITC Fortune and Golden Tree, and to families who want mushrooms that taste the way they should. We have showcased our work with the Uttar Pradesh Department of Horticulture and Food Processing and spoken to agriculture students at G.B. Pant University, Pantnagar.

Values

What we hold ourselves to

  • 01

    Grow it clean

    Natural substrates, heat pasteurisation, filtered air and hygiene at every door, so the crop is healthy without chemical rescue.

  • 02

    Keep it cold

    Same-day dispatch at 2–4 °C. Freshness is a temperature, and we guard it.

  • 03

    Say what it is

    Honest grades, honest weights and straight answers on price and availability.

  • 04

    Share the craft

    Training, talks and an open journal, because North India needs more good mushroom growers.

From the founder
