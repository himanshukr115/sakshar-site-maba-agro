FAQ

Common questions

Where is Maba Agro based?

Our office is at 201, P-6, Ocean Complex, Sector 18, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201301. We grow and dispatch fresh mushrooms to buyers across North India.

Which mushrooms do you grow?

Button (Agaricus bisporus), oyster or dhingri (Pleurotus), shiitake (Lentinula edodes) and portobello. Button is available in the largest volumes; ask us about this week’s harvest for the others.

Are your mushrooms organically grown?

Yes. We grow on natural compost and straw substrates, rely on hygiene, filtered air and climate control instead of chemical shortcuts, and never use preservatives or bleaching agents after harvest.

How should I store fresh mushrooms?

Keep them in the fridge in a paper bag or the original punnet with its film loosened, not in a sealed plastic bag. Do not wash them until just before cooking. Button and portobello keep 5–7 days, oyster 3–5, shiitake up to 10.
