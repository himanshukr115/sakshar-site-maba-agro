FAQ

Wholesale questions

Do you supply sabzi mandi wholesalers?

Yes. We supply graded button mushrooms in bulk to mandi traders and distributors, with daily or alternate-day dispatch depending on your volume and route.

What is the minimum order for wholesale?

It depends on variety and delivery route. Share your expected weekly volume and location in the enquiry form and we will reply with a quote and dispatch schedule.

Do you supply hotels and restaurants?

Yes. Hotel and restaurant kitchens across the region order from us on standing schedules. We can grade, pack and label to your kitchen’s spec.

Can I get a sample before placing a standing order?

For new wholesale and HoReCa partners we arrange a trial lot so your team can check quality, size and shelf life first.

How are mushrooms kept fresh in transit?

Mushrooms are harvested in the early morning, chilled within the hour, packed cold and dispatched the same day, held between 2 and 4 °C wherever possible.
